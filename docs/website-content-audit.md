# DBTA Website Content & Institutional Audit

This document records the comprehensive audit of the official Don Bosco Tech Africa (DBTA) digital presence (reference: `https://dbtechafrica.org/`). It extracts authentic institutional data, structures, governance, networks, and services to serve as the foundation for the new frontend platform.

## 5. Major Programmes, Projects & Initiatives

1. **Green TVET & Renewable Energy Initiative**:
   - Focus: Mainstreaming environmental conservation, climate resilience, Solar PV installation & maintenance, and solar water pumping systems (SPVWPS).
   - Partners: BMZ, Jugend Eine Welt (J1W), Don Bosco Green Alliance.
2. **Agriculture for Life Project**:
   - Focus: Agribusiness training, climate-smart agriculture, production, and sustainable food systems in Salesian communities (e.g., Don Bosco Embu pilot).
3. **Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)**:
   - Focus: Formulating national and regional RPL frameworks to assess and certify informal skills of artisans and youths (Malawi, Burkina Faso, Kenya).
4. **Job Placement & School-to-Work Transition (Inserjeune)**:
   - Focus: Establishing and equipping Job Service Offices (JSOs) across all 119 centres, tracking graduate employment, establishing industry partnerships.
5. **Quality TVET & Capacity Building**:
   - Focus: Quality Management Systems (QMS), instructor continuous pedagogical upgrading, modern curriculum alignment with labour market demands.
6. **Continental TVET Strategy & Policy Advocacy**:
   - Focus: Accelerating the domestication of the African Union Continental TVET Strategy (2025–2034) and hosting the Annual Stakeholders Assembly (ASA).

## 8. Content Audit Findings & Transformation Strategy

1. **Information Architecture Upgrade**:
   - *Old*: Scattered WordPress menus with fragmented external links and orphan posts.
   - *New*: Clean, 8-pillar institutional hierarchy (About, What We Do, Network, Work/Projects, Impact, Knowledge, News/Media, Opportunities, Digital Ecosystem).
2. **Data-Driven Network Explorer**:
   - *Old*: Embedded static Google Map iframe.
   - *New*: Interactive, searchable continental explorer covering 35 countries, 15 provinces, TVET centres, and trade specializations.
3. **Structured Knowledge Hub**:
   - *Old*: Standard download manager list without structured facets.
   - *New*: Multi-filter knowledge catalog (Reports, Research, Toolkits, Policies, Strategic Plans) with real-time topic, country, and year filters.
4. **Storytelling & Editorial Rigour**:
   - *Old*: Simple blog posts.
   - *New*: Dedicated editorial impact stories (Context, Challenge, Intervention, Outcome, Quote, Related Centre & Project).
